Field notes

Think beyond the obvious.

Each flight gives you seven prompts and a short timer. Every valid answer lifts your rocket, and the less obvious it is, the higher it goes: through the sky and the atmosphere, past the Moon, the planets and the Sun, out among the stars and on to the galaxies. Repeats and misses score zero.

How rarity works. Answer pools come from Wikidata. An answer's rarity is how well known it is among everything that fits the prompt, measured by how many Wikipedia language editions have an article about it. Naming the Nile earns little; naming the Vistula earns a lot. When enough players have answered a prompt, real player data is blended in.

Type an answer and press Enter. A near match asks you to confirm, and the timer waits for you.
