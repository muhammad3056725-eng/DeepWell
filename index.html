Field notes

Think beyond the obvious.

Each dive gives you seven prompts and a short timer. Valid answers earn depth. Less-obvious answers earn more. Duplicate answers and misses score zero.

Rarity is estimated from a built-in editorial dataset, not live global player data. The game never pretends otherwise.

Type an answer and press Enter. A near match may ask you to confirm the intended answer without costing time.
